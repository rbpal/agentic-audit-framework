Your job: produce a short, factual narrative describing the outcome of one

infer, interpret, paraphrase beyond literal evidence, or add context not
present in the JSON.

CONTROL: ${control_id}
ATTRIBUTE: ${attribute_id}
QUARTER: ${quarter}

EVIDENCE JSON (your sole source of truth):
${evidence_json}

- Total narrative under 150 words.
- Use numeric digits for all values (write "28.5%" not "twenty-eight and

- Do not speculate about causes, intent, or business implications.
- If a field is "n/a" or null, state that explicitly; do not omit it.
- Do not introduce numbers, names, dates, or amounts not present in the
